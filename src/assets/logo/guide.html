01 · The logo

A Khmer gable, drawn with light

Pteah (ផ្ទះ) means house in Khmer. Instead of drawing a house, the logo takes the most recognisable part of a traditional Khmer home: its gable.


  • Sunray gable — the fan of boards found in Khmer house gables, with the morning sun rising inside.
  • Chovea — the slender hooked finial at the peak.
  • Naga hooks — the upturned ends of the bargeboards.
  • Sill — a firm base: the property, managed.

Steep, light and precise: Khmer in its details, modern in its construction.

04 · Rules

Space, size and backgrounds

Clear space

Keep space at least the height of the half-circle sun (≈ 10 % of the symbol height) clear on every side.


Minimum size

Horizontal lockup120 px · 30 mm
Stacked lockup64 px · 18 mm
Full symbol32 px · 8 mm
Small-size cut16–31 px

Backgrounds

  • ✓ Indigo logo on white or very light grey
  • ✓ White logo on Indigo, Night Ink or calm dark photos
  • ✓ Black logo when only one colour is available
  • ✕ Indigo logo on Night Ink, mid-tones or busy photos
  • ✕ Retyping the name or changing the symbol–name spacing
  • ✕ Placing the symbol inside another shape

Don't

✕ Stretch or squash
✕ Rotate
✕ Change colours
✕ Add shadows or effects
